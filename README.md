## 📌 About the Internship

The **Siemens Data Science Master Virtual Internship** was an 8-week structured learning program conducted under the **AICTE–EduSkills Virtual Internship Program**.

The internship focused on developing industry-oriented knowledge in **Data Science and Machine Learning**, beginning with the identification of business problems suitable for AI/ML and progressing through Data Engineering, data preparation, Machine Learning, advanced Machine Learning techniques, model deployment and monitoring, and AI platform administration.

The program followed a week-wise curriculum consisting of learning modules, weekly assessments, assignments and project documentation. The internship concluded with a **Final Assessment Test** as part of the overall evaluation.

The internship domain mentioned in the official offer letter is **Siemens Data Science Master**, and the internship duration is **June 2026 – August 2026**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
